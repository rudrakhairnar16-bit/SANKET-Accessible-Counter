# External Reviewer Feedback (Informal)

**Status:** Qualitative feedback reported by the project author; not an official evaluation record.

The following summaries reflect feedback from two reviewers in a separate, earlier context. They are not attributed to the current hackathon, and they should not be described as feedback from this hackathon's judges.

## Reviewer 1 — Implementation and inclusive access

The reviewer said the application looked promising and that implementing it sooner could benefit more people. They asked whether a government body would approve or adopt it and suggested exploring one-handed signs where linguistically appropriate, with the aim of supporting people who may have speech or hand-mobility barriers as well as Deaf users.

## Reviewer 2 — Problem relevance and potential impact

The reviewer felt the concept addressed a genuine problem and proposed a solution that could help people facing communication barriers. They described their experience with the concept positively and said the application could be useful to people who need it.

## Limitations and use in submissions

- These are paraphrases of feedback reported by the project author, not verbatim quotations or independently verified transcripts.
- Do not identify the reviewers as current-hackathon judges, government officials, sign-language experts, or Deaf/Hard-of-Hearing participants unless independently confirmed.
- This feedback is not evidence of government approval, sign-language accuracy, accessibility certification, or real-user testing.
- Use as **informal external reviewer feedback** only, and obtain permission before sharing identifying details or verbatim quotes.
- The one-handed-sign suggestion is a design hypothesis to explore with qualified ISL experts and users with varied access needs. Do not assume that one-handed signs exist for every concept or are universally accessible.
