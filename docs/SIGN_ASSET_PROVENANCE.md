# SANKET Sign Asset Provenance

## Current verified source references

### ASL alphabet
The A–Z references use Wikimedia Commons files sourced from the public-domain wpclipart ASL alphabet. The Commons pages identify the works as public domain. Example references: `Sign language A.svg` and `Sign language Z.svg`.

### ASL civic/basic references
A small subset of the 10 ASL counter basics now has traceable Wikimedia Commons reference images (Hello, Yes, No, Please, Thank You, I Understand). Licensing varies by asset and must be preserved with attribution/share-alike terms where applicable. Examples include Wikimedia Commons references for Yes, No, Please, Thank You, and Understand.

## ISL restriction
The ISL municipal inventory remains **unbundled**. The source metadata points toward ISLRTC/ISL datasets, but a source mention is not the same thing as redistribution permission. Until explicit redistribution rights are obtained and the meaning is reviewed by a qualified ISL expert, the UI keeps the placeholder state.

## Release rule
A sign may move from `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` to production-approved only after: (1) source/license evidence is recorded, (2) the exact meaning is checked by a qualified language reviewer, and (3) the asset is stable at the deployed URL or bundled locally.
