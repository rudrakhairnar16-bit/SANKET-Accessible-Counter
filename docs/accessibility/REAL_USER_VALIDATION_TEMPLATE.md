## 1. Ethical Guidelines & Participant Protection

Conducting usability studies with Deaf, hard-of-hearing, and disabled participants requires rigorous ethical planning, respectful communication channels, and strict privacy protection:

1. **Informed Consent in Primary Language:** Information sheets and consent forms must be provided in written plain language and through an on-screen or in-person certified Indian Sign Language (ISL) interpreter.
2. **Voluntary Participation & Withdrawal:** Participants may pause, skip tasks, or withdraw consent at any time without forfeiture of compensation or civic standing.
3. **No Unconsented Recording:** Video or facial recording is strictly optional and requires separate, explicit opt-in consent. Zero biometric data is gathered during standard SANKET sessions.
4. **Fair Compensation:** Participants must be compensated equitably for their specialized linguistic insight and time.
5. **Certified ISL Coordination:** Sessions must be co-facilitated by a certified ISL interpreter or a Deaf community liaison to eliminate communication barriers.

## 4. Standard Usability Tasks & Evaluation Scoring

### Task 1: Interface Orientation & Accessibility Customization
* **Prompt:** "Adjust the display so it is most comfortable for your reading (e.g., text size, contrast, or sign language selection)."
* **Metrics:** Time to complete (seconds), number of navigation errors, ease rating (1-5).
* **Observation Focus:** Did the user easily locate the accessibility toolbar? Was the language toggle clear?

### Task 2: Civic Query Composition via Sign Selector
* **Prompt:** "Imagine you need to ask the counter clerk about submitting a certificate form. Find the signs for this query and prepare them to show the staff."
* **Metrics:** Time to assemble draft sequence, number of incorrect sign clicks, draft tray edit actions.
* **Observation Focus:** Did the sign category icons make sense? Were the sign glosses understandable? Did the user understand that the current graphics are placeholders?

### Task 3: Transmission & Staff Interaction
* **Prompt:** "Send your drafted message to the staff counter and review their response."
* **Metrics:** Success (Yes/No), comprehension of incoming staff message, subjective satisfaction.
* **Observation Focus:** Was the conversation timeline clear? Did the citizen realize their message was delivered?
